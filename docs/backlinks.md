- **Root Domain Link:** Add a clear, permanent footer and project showcase link on `https://sooubh.in` pointing directly to `https://nashik.sooubh.in`.
- **Google Play Store Listing:** In the Trivio app metadata on Google Play Console, list `https://nashik.sooubh.in` as the official application website and privacy policy host.
