## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Is the TRIVIO app free to download and use?
**A:** Yes! TRIVIO is 100% free to download on Android via the Google Play Store. You get full access to all destination guides, category listings, offline caching, and 3 complimentary AI-generated multi-day itineraries with zero upfront cost.

### Q2: How does TRIVIO's AI Trip Planner differ from ChatGPT or Google Maps?
**A:** Generic AI chatbots often hallucinate places that are closed, suggest unrealistic travel times, or pair destinations on opposite sides of Nashik. TRIVIO's proprietary domain engine runs deterministic constraint validation. It strictly checks physical opening/closing hours, calculates real Haversine road travel speeds, injects curated lunch and dinner stops, and ensures your schedule is physically feasible.

### Q3: Does TRIVIO work offline without mobile data or Wi-Fi?
**A:** Absolutely. We built TRIVIO local-first. Once you open the app, essential place details, historical guides, emergency contact numbers, and your saved wishlist boards are cached locally on your device via high-speed Hive storage. You can navigate Sahyadri mountain trails with zero cellular bars.

### Q4: Are the temple timings and dress codes up to date?
**A:** Yes. Our local Nashik administrative team and community moderators regularly verify temple aarti schedules, VIP darshan counter fees, and sanctum dress code regulations (such as traditional attire requirements for Trimbakeshwar and Kalaram temples).

### Q5: Can I customize the pace and budget of my itinerary?
**A:** Yes. When generating a trip in TRIVIO, you can choose between Relaxed (2–3 stops per day), Moderate (4–5 stops per day), or Packed (6–7 stops per day) pacing. You can also specify your budget tier (Budget, Balanced, or Luxury) to receive personalized dining and stay suggestions.

### Q6: Can I use TRIVIO to book wine tastings at Sula or York?
**A:** TRIVIO provides complete, verified booking telephone numbers, official reservation websites, tasting session hours, and entry fee breakdowns for all leading wineries in Gangapur, Dindori, and Sanjegaon. You can initiate reservations or call tasting rooms with a single tap.

### Q7: Is an iOS (iPhone) version of TRIVIO available?
**A:** TRIVIO is built on Flutter with full cross-platform architecture. While the initial public release is focused on Android via Google Play, an iOS version for the Apple App Store is currently in internal testing and will be released soon. You can use our web trip planner on iOS browsers in the meantime.

### Q8: How can I report an incorrect place listing or suggest a hidden gem?
**A:** Inside the app, every place page includes a "Report an Issue" button where you can submit corrections regarding timings, parking, or facilities. To submit a new local attraction or partner your tourism business, email us at `trivio-support@sooubh.in`.
