# Nashik Travel Guide (Trivio) Content Guidelines

## 1. Ground Truth & Zero Hallucination Policy
- **Zero invented facts.** Every date, timing, fee, distance, rule, contact number, route, and historical statistic must be cross-verified live against primary sources:
  - Maharashtra Tourism (MTDC)
  - Nashik Municipal Corporation (NMC)
  - Simhastha Kumbh Mela Authority & Nashik District Collectorate
  - Shri Trimbakeshwar Devasthan Trust & Kalaram Sansthan
  - Indian Railways (IRCTC / Central Railway) & MSRTC
  - Archaeological Survey of India (ASI)
- If a rule or future schedule is unannounced (e.g. specific special train numbers for 2027), explicitly write "Official notification awaited; check official portal" or omit.

## 2. Article Structure Standard
Every article must follow this layout:
1. **Title & Frontmatter**: Clear, search-intent driven title (50-60 characters) and description (140-160 characters).
2. **Direct Answer Summary (GEO / AI citation block)**: 40-50 words answer-first summary at the top covering key practical facts (timings, costs, location, booking requirement).
3. **Structured Body**:
   - Clear H2/H3 headings
   - Key practical details in Markdown tables (Timings, Entry Fees, Transit options)
   - Do's and Don'ts / Dress codes / Safety advisories
   - Real FAQ accordion with schema markup
4. **Contextual Internal Links**: 3-5 links to related places, guides, or hub categories with descriptive anchors.
5. **Trivio App CTA**: Contextual call-to-action with UTM parameters (`utm_source=website&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=<slug>`).
6. **Sources & Last Verified**:
   - Full list of verified official URLs
   - Visible "Last verified: YYYY-MM-DD" stamp.

## 3. SEO & Entity Standards
- Canonical format: `https://nashik.sooubh.in/<category>/<slug>`
- Multilingual parity: If an article is published in English, corresponding Marathi (`/mr/`) and Hindi (`/hi/`) versions must contain identical factual numbers and verified dates.
- High-intent entity consistency:
  - "Trimbakeshwar Shiva Temple" (Jyotirlinga)
  - "Simhastha Kumbh Mela Nashik-Trimbakeshwar"
  - "Panchavati & Kalaram Temple"
  - "Sula Vineyards & Gangapur Dam"
  - "Harihar Fort & Sahyadri Trek"
