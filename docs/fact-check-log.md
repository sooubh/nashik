## 6. Discrepancy Reconciliation Log

During this audit cycle, the following claims were scrutinized and resolved against ground truth:

1. **Saptashrungi Ropeway Tariff**:
   - *Previous Reference*: In early drafts, a generic ₹120 round-trip fare was referenced.
   - *Official Resolution*: Cross-verified against the official concessionaire Suyog Gurbaxani Funicular Ropeways Ltd (SGFRL) and Shri Saptashrung Nivasini Devi Trust. The gazetted return tariff is ₹110 for adults and ₹55 for children. All pages and guides updated to reflect ₹110 return (adults) / ₹55 (children).
2. **Sula Vineyards Entry Cover vs. Wine Tasting Packages**:
   - *Previous Reference*: Drafts noted a ₹400 standard tasting fee without clear distinction of gate cover.
   - *Official Resolution*: Sula Vineyards operates a mandatory gate cover of ₹600 (weekdays) or ₹1,000 (weekends), which is 100% redeemable across restaurants, retail, and tasting rooms. Guided tasting of 6 wines costs ₹600 (weekdays) or ₹700 (weekends). Clarified explicitly across `/places/sula-vineyards` and `/guides/sula-vineyards-tour-tasting-complete-guide`.
3. **Simhastha Kumbh Mela Timing and Shahi Snan Kunds**:
   - *Previous Reference*: Ambiguous mention of a single bathing site.
   - *Official Resolution*: Re-verified against the historical 1772 Peshwa Madhavrao treaty and Akhada Parishad charters: Vaishnava Akhadas bathe exclusively at Ramkund (Nashik city), while Shaiva Akhadas bathe at Kushavarta Kund (Trimbakeshwar).
