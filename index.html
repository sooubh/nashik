Technical Implementation

The application is written in **Flutter** using **Riverpod** state management and code generation (`build_runner`). All data resides dynamically in **Cloud Firestore** cached locally with **Hive** DB.

  • Local caching with Hive DB
  • Riverpod UI state flow
  • Google Maps intent redirection
  • Secured Google Sign-In backend

About Nashik Travel Guide

Explore the Wine Capital & Spiritual Heart of India

Nashik is a city of rich culture, spiritual depth, and scenic wonders. From the sacred waters of Godavari in Trimbakeshwar to the sprawling hills of Anjaneri and the premium wineries of Sula, there is something for every traveler.

**Nashik Travel Guide** is designed to compile all tourist insights in one clean, unified directory. No more scouring fragmented travel websites or dealing with outdated timetables. Our application is built by local explorers to provide direct location guides, travel routes, difficulty ratings, and safety alerts.
