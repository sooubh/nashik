Delete Your Nashik Explore Account

At Nashik Explore, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. If you wish to permanently delete your account, you may submit an account deletion request using the instructions below.

Important:
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your request has been completed, you will lose access to your account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to support@yourdomain.com.
  2. Use the subject: Delete My Nashik Explore Account
  3. Include the email address associated with your account.
  4. If available, include your User ID.
  5. Our support team may contact you to verify ownership of the account before processing the request.
Request Account Deletion

What Data Will Be Deleted?

The following information will be permanently deleted:

  • Your account profile.
  • Name.
  • Email address.
  • Profile picture.
  • Saved preferences.
  • Bookmarks and favourite places.
  • Saved itineraries and trips.
  • Reviews and ratings (if applicable).
  • Uploaded content associated with your account.
  • Other personal information linked to your account.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where required for:

  • Legal compliance.
  • Fraud prevention.
  • Security investigations.
  • Tax or financial obligations (if applicable).
  • Backup recovery for a limited duration.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are normally processed within 7 business days. In some situations, verification may require additional time.

Need Help?

If you have any questions regarding account deletion or privacy, please contact:

Email: support@yourdomain.com

Website: https://yourdomain.com

Warning
Deleting your account is permanent. After deletion you cannot recover your account, saved trips, preferences, or any associated personal information.
