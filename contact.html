Contact & Support

Last Updated: July 14, 2026

1. Support Channels

For support regarding user profiles, deleting database records, reporting wrong coordinates, or seeking help with Premium membership status, contact us at:
support@nashikexplore.com

2. Response SLA

Our helpdesk monitors incoming emails from Monday through Saturday between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM IST (GMT+5:30). General queries receive replies within 24 to 48 business hours. Account data deletes or subscription queries receive high-priority processing.

3. Feedback Moderation

In compliance with our review submission rules (rating values between 1.0 and 5.0, comment lengths <= 1000 characters), users can report inappropriate reviews directly in the App. Reports (reports collection) will be processed by administrators.
