Cookies Policy

Last Updated: July 14, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are simple text blocks sent to browser directories. Mobile applications do not load cookie headers. Instead, they write to local sandboxed databases and persistent preferences to manage your logged-in session.

2. Website LocalStorage

Our landing website does not load external cookie files or trackers. We only access the HTML5 localStorage API to store the key color-theme (set to 'dark' or 'light') so your theme selection persists between visits.

3. Browser Cookies Disclaimers

Browser cookies are typically used to track sessions across different websites. We do not use browser tracking cookies or analytics beacons on this static website. It is 100% free of invasive user tracking.

4. Android Application Storage

The Android application does not run or parse web browser cookies. All local state caches are handled in secure sandboxed files using the following structures:

  • Firebase Auth Keychain: Native secure keychain tokens persist your authenticated login session state.
  • Hive local db sandbox:
    • user_profile_box: Caches your user settings under the 'profile' key.
    • wishlist_boards_{userId}: Caches your custom travel boards and bookmarked place IDs offline.

5. Advertising Identifiers

Free-tier users see ads sourced from Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). AdMob uses your device's Mobile Advertising ID (Google AdID) to personalize banners. Upon upgrading to Premium (₹199 lifetime fee), the AdMob initialization routine is disengaged, stopping ad-related data lookups.
