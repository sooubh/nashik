Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 14, 2026

Policy Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the Nashik Explore application (registered in the manifest as the "Nashik Travel Guide" mobile app). We detail what information is collected, what permissions are active, where your variables are stored, and how you can exercise account controls.

1. Data We Collect

The application collects and processes specific data inputs to run account synchronizations and community directories:

  • Authentication Metadata: Display name, email address, profile photo URL, and secure session tokens generated during Google Sign-In or Firebase email login.
  • Preferences & Setup Metrics: Optional user phone number, city setting (defaults to "Nashik"), dark mode preferences, and notification toggles.
  • User-Generated Travel Logs: Wishlist boards (arrays of place identifier strings), ratings (from 1.0 to 5.0), commentary text (limited to 1000 characters), and reported issues.
  • Usage Counts: Generated trip counts, indicating how many itineraries you have created.
  • Device Ad Identifiers: The Google Advertising ID (AdID) processed on the free tier to present customized advertisements.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We confirm that the application does **not** collect or record:

  • Device GPS Coordinates: The app does not read your device's geographical GPS coordinates. Distances (e.g. coordinates from Ozar Airport) are calculated statically relative to hardcoded coordinates.
  • Camera sensor feeds: The app does not request camera permissions. Photo selection uses the gallery picker interface only.
  • Sensitive hardware directories: Contacts, call lists, calendar entries, or microphone inputs.

3. Permissions Disclosures

The application manifest declares the following permissions. We disclose their status below:

Permission Status Actual Purpose
INTERNET Active To contact Firebase DB, sync wishlists, and retrieve advertisements.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Active To monitor online states and direct requests to local Hive database storage.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Inactive / Manifest Only Declared in the manifest but **never** accessed by the application's compiled code.
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Inactive / Manifest Only Declared in the manifest but **never** accessed by the application's compiled code.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Inactive / Manifest Only Declared in the manifest but **never** accessed by native background services.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your records are stored across three main structures:

  • Local Hive Sandbox: Caches settings and user boards on the device sandbox directory. Caching files are not encrypted at the app layer (no custom encryption keys are specified in Hive declarations).
  • Cloud Firestore & Authentication: Houses secure email credentials, profiles, wishlist documents, and review logs. Communication uses HTTPS transport layer security (TLS/SSL).
  • Firebase Storage: Houses custom profile pictures.

5. Third-Party Networks

We integrate with the following third-party services:

Partner Service Data Processed Purpose
Google Firebase Authentication parameters, profiles, reviews, wishlists, and profile pictures. Core cloud database hosting and user login authentication.
Google AdMob Google Advertising ID (AdID). Delivers target advertisements to free users. Deactivated on Premium.
RevenueCat In-app purchase validation keys. Validates Premium upgrades. No credit card details are processed.
Google Maps None. Redirection only. Launches native Google Maps app to view destinations.

6. Account Deletion & Limits

Tapping "Delete Account" executes the client-side deleteAccount() method. This deletes the core user profile document /users/{uid}, deletes your login authentication node, and clears the local Hive cache.
Remaining Data Limitations: The client-side database wipe does not automatically trigger the deletion of subcollections (your reviews list /users/{uid}/reviews and boards /users/{uid}/boards) or your profile picture in Firebase Storage. If you seek absolute erasure of all reviews and media, please submit a deletion request via our Data Safety web tool.

7. Your User Rights

Under data protection guidelines, you retain the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can view all saved wishlist boards and reviews directly in the App interface.
  • Right to Edit: You can edit your display name, optional phone number, and avatar image at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request account termination and subsequent manual database cleanups.

8. Children's Privacy

Nashik Explore does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and determine that a child has created an account, please contact us at support@nashikexplore.com to delete the account record.
