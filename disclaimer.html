1. Destination Information

Timings, entry pricing, accessibility markers, reviews, and general route listings are compiled directly from database records. Local conditions, temple timings, and winery entry rules change dynamically. Users must verify information locally before starting journeys. We accept no responsibility for closures or incorrect details.

2. Trekking & Outdoor Safety

Outdoor destinations listed (such as waterfalls like Dugarwadi or climbs like Harihar Fort) present physical hazards:

  • Climbing Risks: Scaling fort stairs (e.g. steep rock steps at Harihar Fort) requires physical preparation. Treks require careful planning. Difficulty ratings are subjective and based on average conditions.
  • Monsoon Hazards: Waterfall paths and steep stairways become extremely slick and dangerous during the monsoon months (June to September) due to flash floods and mudslides. Check local government alerts and forestry guidelines before starting.
  • No Liability: Nashik Travel Guide is not responsible for injuries, falls, or incidents occurring at any listed destination.

3. Vineyard Laws & Compliance

Our database lists local vineyards (e.g. Sula, York). Visitors must comply with Maharashtra state legal drinking age restrictions (25 years). We do not promote drinking and driving, and encourage hiring local taxi drivers or nominating a designated driver when visiting vineyards.

4. Maps and GPS Limitations

Maps and navigation links redirect users to native Google Maps. We are not liable for routing errors, signal drops, or blocked rural pathways. In rural areas surrounding Nashik (near forts and waterfalls), GPS coordinates may direct users through unpaved terrains. Verify routing safety with local residents.

5. Caching Weather Data

The weather widget reads a cached document updated hourly via Cloud Functions. It is a general snapshot and is not meant to be used for emergency storm warnings, monsoonal flash-flood tracking, or extreme weather preparations. Refer to the Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) for safety warnings.
